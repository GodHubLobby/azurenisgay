01 — What AutoMM is

A middleman that never logs off.

On a trading server, the riskiest moment is always the same one: someone has to send first. AutoMM exists to remove that risk entirely.

AutoMM is a self-contained escrow system built into the RBZ Plaza Discord. When two people want to trade, they open a private deal ticket instead of DMing each other and hoping for the best.

Inside that ticket, both sides agree on terms, pick who's sending and who's receiving, and settle on an amount. From there, AutoMM takes over — it generates a real payment invoice, watches the blockchain for the deposit to actually clear, and only releases funds once the sender confirms they're satisfied.

If anything looks off at any point, a staff member can step into the ticket directly — automation runs the deal, it doesn't run away from it.

02 — How the deal flow works

Eight steps. Same order, every time.

The whole point of automating this is that it never skips a step under pressure. Here's exactly what happens between opening a ticket and funds landing.

01OPEN

A private ticket is created

Either side presses "Request a Middleman" from the panel. AutoMM spins up a private channel just for this deal, tagged with its own deal ID.

02CONFIRM

Both parties confirm and agree to terms

The other side is tagged in. Both people have to actively press confirm before anything moves forward — no deal proceeds on one person's word alone.

03ROLES

Sender and receiver are chosen

Each side picks a role. The two roles can't match — there's always exactly one sender and one receiver on record.

04AMOUNT

The deal amount is set and double-checked

The sender enters the USD amount. The receiver has to confirm it's correct before the deal can continue.

Both sides sign off
05PAYOUT ADDR

The receiver adds a payout address

Where the funds should land once the deal is done — set once, up front, before any money moves.

06INVOICE

AutoMM generates a deposit invoice

A unique deposit address is created for this deal only, through BTCPay. The sender gets the exact address and amount to send.

07WATCH

The chain is watched, not trusted on word

AutoMM polls the invoice directly — it waits for the deposit to actually clear before telling anyone the deal is ready.

No "I already sent it, trust me"
08RELEASE

Sender releases, payout goes out automatically

Once the sender is satisfied, one button triggers the payout straight to the receiver's address. The full transcript stays logged.

03 — Why it holds up

Built for the deal that goes wrong,
not just the one that goes right.

Anyone can build a happy path. What actually earns trust on a trading server is what happens when something doesn't line up.

Never off the clock

A human middleman sleeps, has school, has a life. AutoMM opens tickets and generates invoices at 4am the same way it does at 4pm.

Nothing is taken on word

Deposits are confirmed on-chain before the deal moves forward. "I sent it" isn't a status — a cleared transaction is.

Staff can always step in

Automation runs the routine steps, but it's not the last word. If anything looks wrong, a staff member is one ping away, in the same ticket.

Every deal leaves a record

Deal ID, amounts, addresses, timestamps — logged and kept, not scattered across DMs that disappear when someone leaves the server.

One deal, one address

No shared or recycled wallets. Every deal gets its own deposit address, so funds are never sitting in a general pool.

Built to grow with the server

The same ticket-and-invoice system scales from one deal a night to dozens running in parallel, without needing more people watching over it.
